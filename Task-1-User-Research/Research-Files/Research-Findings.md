# ShopEase – Research Findings

## Research Summary
An online survey was conducted to understand customer shopping behaviour.

## Participants
Total Responses: 8

## Key Research Areas
- Age groups of online shoppers
- Customer occupation
- Online shopping frequency
- Preferred shopping platforms
- Common shopping difficulties
- Customer expectations

## Identified UX Improvement Areas
1. Better product images and descriptions.
2. Improved product search and navigation.
3. Secure payment experience.
4. Easy return and refund process.
5. Better delivery experience.

## Conclusion
The research helps identify customer needs and
provides a foundation for improving the ShopEase
e-commerce user experience.
