# Empathy Map – ShopEase

## 1. What Does the User Say?
- Online shopping should be easy.
- Product details must be accurate.
- Delivery should be faster.

## 2. What Does the User Think?
- Is this product worth buying?
- Is the website trustworthy?
- Will the product match the images?

## 3. What Does the User Feel?
- Happy when discounts are available.
- Worried about payment security.
- Disappointed with delayed delivery.

## 4. What Does the User Do?
- Compares products.
- Checks customer reviews.
- Searches for discounts.
- Tracks orders.

## 5. User Pain Points
- Poor product images.
- Delayed delivery.
- Complicated returns.
- Unclear product descriptions.

## 6. User Needs
- Secure payments.
- Accurate product details.
- Easy returns.
- Faster delivery.
