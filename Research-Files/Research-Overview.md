# ShopEase – User Research

## Project Overview
ShopEase is an e-commerce platform focused on improving the online shopping experience.

## Research Objective
- Understand customer shopping behaviour.
- Identify difficulties faced by online shoppers.
- Understand customer expectations.
- Improve user experience.

## Research Method
Online survey using Google Forms.

## Participants
Total Responses Collected: 7

## Research Focus
- Shopping preferences
- Payment experience
- Product search
- Delivery experience
- Customer satisfaction
